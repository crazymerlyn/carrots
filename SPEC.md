Carrots
=======

A card game with 2 or more players.

Each splayer starts with 4 cards(carrots) in a two by two grid face down in front of them.
The players can look at the bottom two cards.

There's a deck and a discard pile with the top card visible.

On your turn, you chose to take a card, either from the deck or from the discard pile.
Upon looking at the card, you can replace one of your carrots with it, playing the new card face down in the same position. You discard the carrot you replaced.
If you took the card from the deck, you can chose to discard it instead.


Powers
------
Certain cards have powers. They are executed when a player discards that card.
The powers are:
- 7 - Look at one of your carrots.
- 8 - Look at one of another player's carrots.
- 9 - Swap any two carrots in game without looking.

When a player discards a card as part of their turn, any player (including themselves) can discard a matching carrot.
Just flip a carrot and discard it. If the carrot matches the card, you get to lose a carrot. If not, you get two penalty carrots from the deck without looking.

At the end of your turn, you can chose to claim that your carrots are cooked.
Once a player claims that, the game proceeds as normal until the player gets a turn again. That's the final turn of the game.
At the end of the game, the player with the lowest score wins.

Score
-----

Number cards have a score of their value.
Face cards are valued at 10. Aces are 1.
The exception to this is Red Kings, which are -1.
